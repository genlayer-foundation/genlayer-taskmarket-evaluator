What both parties can verify

Who judged the work

GenLayer selects the jury from validators who stake tokens. They earn rewards for participating and risk their stake if they break the rules. This jury stakes test GEN. You can inspect all five votes in the first case.

What each milestone earned

The buyer prices each milestone up front. Each gets its own ruling, so a worker can earn a partial payment when only some milestones pass.

Where the funds went

Each completed case links the jury’s ruling to a Tempo receipt. The buyer and worker can check the exact amount paid or refunded.

For buyers

Hire Internet Court

New pilot tasks carry a 0% evaluator fee. Taskmarket charges its own action fees. The listed 24 hours is the deadline to post a verdict after delivery.

What can the jury read?

By default the judges read the submitted files. If a claim depends on a website, agree on its domain before posting and enable web evidence for that milestone. Cite one to three public HTTPS pages in the rubric or submission. Each judge opens them independently and reads the first 6,000 characters of visible text per page.

New milestones pass or fail. A worker earns payment by showing that the agreed conditions were met. Missing required proof fails the milestone. If the evaluator cannot retrieve a page or reach a valid ruling, settlement pauses. Use a stable receipt or record anyone can open. Its web access is limited to cited pages. See the jury verify a public record.

Our first Bradbury testnet case had a 60-second appeal window. GenLayer mainnet will give each decision a fixed 30-minute appeal window. A challenge brings the case to another round.

  1. Agree the terms

    Contact Internet Court before posting. Agree on the evidence the jury should look for and what each milestone pays. Read the mainnet pilot agreement.

  2. Select the evaluator

    Choose Internet Court on Tempo mainnet for real OUSD or Tempo testnet for test OUSD. Include the agreed terms and share the task ID to complete enrollment.

  3. Follow the jury live

    The buyer and worker each need a registered Taskmarket email inbox. Both receive a case link before judging starts. Keep it open for the ruling ID, provisional decision, reasons and exact appeal deadline.

  4. Challenge a provisional ruling

    Either party can ask a larger jury to review the decision. Prepare a Bradbury wallet with test GEN for the bond, extra jury work and gas. An unsuccessful challenge can cost you the bond. The chain must include the appeal before the deadline. Read the GenLayer appeal rules.

    Taskmarket has its own appeal window after the GenLayer ruling is final. Name a dispute resolver when posting to enable worker appeals there.
