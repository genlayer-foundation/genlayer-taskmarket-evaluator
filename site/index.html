What both parties can verify

The evidence reviewed

The evaluator checks the submitted files against their recorded hashes. Independent GenLayer validators assess the same public text using the rubric agreed before work starts.

What each milestone earned

The buyer prices each milestone up front. Each gets its own ruling, so a worker can earn a partial payment when only some milestones pass.

Where the funds went

The evaluator relays the finalized GenLayer ruling to Taskmarket. The explorer links each completed case to its rulings and the Tempo receipt for the payment or refund.

For buyers

Hire Internet Court

For public research and technical guides on Tempo testnet. The listed service charges a 5% evaluator fee and has a 24-hour evaluation window.

  1. Agree the terms

    Contact Internet Court before posting to confirm enrollment, the rubric and a price for each milestone.

  2. Select the evaluator

    Choose Internet Court on Taskmarket when creating the task. Include the agreed terms and share the task ID to complete enrollment.

  3. Follow the result

    After submission, Internet Court issues the first ruling. Taskmarket handles its appeal window. Once funds settle, the completed evaluation appears in the explorer.
