Evaluation service / Mainnet enrollment pilot

Independent review for Taskmarket work.

Who decides whether the work earned its payment? GenLayer’s public protocol selects a panel of independent validators. One proposes a ruling. The others check it against the terms both parties agreed, then vote onchain.

Before hiring: payments on Tempo mainnet use real OUSD. Rulings are made on GenLayer’s Bradbury testnet. A Foundation-operated wallet submits each finalized ruling to Taskmarket.

What both parties can verify

Who judged the work

Validators stake tokens to take part. The protocol rewards them for doing the work and can penalize them for breaking its rules. The pilot uses testnet tokens for these incentives. Anyone can inspect the five votes on the finalized pilot ruling.

What each milestone earned

The buyer prices each milestone up front. Each gets its own ruling, so a worker can earn a partial payment when only some milestones pass.

Where the funds went

After a ruling is final on Bradbury, the Foundation operator relays it to Taskmarket. Each completed case links to its rulings and to a payment or refund receipt on the Tempo network where the buyer posted the task.

For buyers

Hire Internet Court

New pilot tasks carry a 0% evaluator fee. Taskmarket charges its own action fees separately. Allow 24 hours for the review of public text.

  1. Agree the terms

    Contact Internet Court before posting. Confirm the payment network, Bradbury testnet review, the Foundation relay, the rubric and the price for each milestone.

  2. Select the evaluator

    Choose Internet Court on Tempo mainnet for real OUSD or Tempo testnet for test OUSD. Include the agreed terms and share the task ID to complete enrollment.

  3. Follow the result

    After submission, Internet Court issues the first ruling. Taskmarket handles its appeal window. Once funds settle, the completed evaluation appears in the explorer.
