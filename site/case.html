Ask a larger jury to review a ruling

Either the buyer or the worker can challenge a provisional ruling. A fresh jury reviews the decision and can overturn it. An eligible appeal is open to anyone on GenLayer.

  1. Before judging begins, prepare a Bradbury wallet with test GEN. The appeal quote covers a bond and the extra jury work. Keep some test GEN for gas. A failed challenge can cost you the bond.
  2. When the jury decides, check the verdict, its reasons and the UTC deadline above. The first pilot allowed just 60 seconds. Keep this page open because inbox delivery can take longer.
  3. To challenge, use the ruling’s GenLayer transaction ID and your funded wallet. Check the current quote first. The chain must include the appeal before the deadline. How GenLayer appeals work

After GenLayer finalizes, Taskmarket handles payment. Its separate worker appeal requires a dispute resolver named when the task was posted.
